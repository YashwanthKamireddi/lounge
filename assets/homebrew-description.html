A calm, tvOS-style home screen for rooted LG webOS TVs, native and light.

A dock, app grid and Top Shelf art in Liquid Glass, light or dark. Move or hide any app or HDMI input, switch between open apps, search, and add weather for a city of your choice. Works with the remote's arrows or the Magic Remote pointer. Behind an app it does no work and frees its memory, so streaming apps get the whole TV.

Requires a rooted TV and elevated Homebrew Channel. English only.
