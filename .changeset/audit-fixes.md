Holding OK on an app opens Move, Hide and Uninstall again (the LG remote has no Menu key, so Hide and Uninstall were out of reach). Move's hint follows the app being moved instead of covering the dock. An app has one name everywhere. Settings pages are titled for what they show, inputs appear and disappear on their own when a device is plugged in, and the manual refresh rows and the Quit row (as the TV's Home) are gone. The app switcher says what OK, Up and Back do.
