A Search button at the top left and an Inputs button beside the time: press Up from the top row of apps to reach the one above it. Menus, the Inputs panel, the uninstall alert and Move's hint share the dock's clear glass, and hiding or uninstalling an app lets it shrink away before the others close the gap.
