Your own wallpaper and profile picture, sent from your phone. Settings › Background ends with a "From your phone" tile, and Profile picture has the same row: the TV shows a QR code, your phone opens a small page, you pick a photo and tap Send. The phone fits the photo itself (iPhone HEIC included) and the TV makes the wallpaper's shading and glass from it, as for the bundled ones. The TV only listens while the code is on screen, on your own network, at an address with a random token.
