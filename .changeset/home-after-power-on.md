After a cold start, Lounge comes up over the TV's input even when webOS opens that input late, after Home is already up. It follows the TV's own Home Auto Launch setting, as LG's Home does: with it off, the TV stays on its input. The "Make Lounge your Home" question waits until the screen has settled, and alerts and menus open with their button already in place, so they fade in without a shake.
