Lounge as Home can always be switched off with the remote. A change that went through is no longer reported as failed: when a reply is lost while Home switches over, Lounge reads the TV's real state before saying anything. While Lounge is the TV's Home, the Settings row always shows On and turns it off, and a row that couldn't read the TV reads it again when pressed instead of staying "Not available". A detail of the TV that can't be read no longer fails the whole check.
